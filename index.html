The leak

Private dentistry doesn't lose money in the chair. It loses it before hello.

Lost enquiries leave no trace — which is why no practice owner can tell you their real number. That silence is the most expensive thing in your business.

01

Missed calls vanish

The phone rings out at lunch, after five, on Saturday. The patient books with whoever answers next. There is no record it ever happened.

02

Slow replies lose intent

An implant enquiry answered in five minutes and one answered tomorrow are two different businesses. High-intent patients shop two or three clinics — first credible response usually wins.

03

DMs nobody owns

Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, website chat. Enquiries scattered across inboxes with no owner, no priority, no clock.

04

NHS and private blur together

Mixed clinics can't see which enquiries are contract demand, which are private revenue — and which NHS patient just quietly asked about whitening.

05

£18,000 filed as admin

A full-arch enquiry and a reschedule land in the same inbox and get the same priority: none. Your inbox doesn't know what anything is worth.

06

Owners see half the picture

Your PMS shows what was booked. It will never show what was asked for and lost — and that's the number that decides your growth.

FAQ

Fair questions

Does AI talk to my patients?

Only with wording your dentist has approved, only for enquiry handling, never for anything clinical. Urgent cases go straight to humans. Until you approve wording, Cura runs in capture-and-route mode: your team replies, Cura makes sure nothing is missed.

We already have a receptionist.

Keep them — Cura makes them dangerous. It catches what a desk physically can't: the 7pm form, the Saturday missed call, the Instagram DM. Your team gets a ranked task list instead of a guessing game.

We're a mixed NHS/private practice.

So is our pilot clinic. Cura separates private revenue, NHS demand and mixed opportunities — NHS enquiries tracked for your contract, and the private interest hiding inside them stops slipping through.

We use Dentally / another PMS.

Cura works in front of your PMS, not instead of it. Patients still book into your existing diary. PMS sync — starting with Dentally — is on the roadmap to close the loop automatically.

What about GDPR?

Patient data lives in a locked-down database with zero public access, encrypted in transit, full audit trail. Marketing contact only happens with recorded consent, and opt-outs are enforced instantly and permanently.

How do we know it makes money?

You watch your own numbers: missed calls captured, response times, pipeline value created, revenue at risk recovered — from week one, on real enquiries. We don't ask you to believe a case study; we show you your own front door.

How long does setup take?

The system deploys in days. Go-live is gated on the only thing that should gate it: your dentist approving how patients are spoken to.
